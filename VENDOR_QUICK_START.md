# Vendor Panel - Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started as a Vendor

### Step 1: Account Creation & Approval
1. Request vendor account from Secure Seat administration
2. Provide the following information:
   - Business name
   - Business type (Entertainment, Dining, Religious, Workshop, Travel, Attraction)
   - Business license number
   - Tax ID
   - Contact person details
   - Bank account information

3. Your account will be assigned a **Pending** status
4. Admin team will review and approve within 2-3 business days
5. You'll receive an email notification once approved

### Step 2: First Login
1. Go to `https://secureseat.com/vendor/dashboard`
2. Log in with your email and password
3. Complete your profile information:
   - Phone number
   - Full address
   - Business description
4. Click "Save Changes"

### Step 3: Create Your First Experience

#### Option A: Instant Booking (Full Payment)
Best for: Movies, Concerts, Events where users pay full price upfront

1. Go to **Your Experiences** → **Create Experience**
2. Fill in basic details:
   - Title: "Concert Night - Live Band"
   - Category: Entertainment
   - Location: Your venue address
   - Description: Event details
   - Highlights: Key features (comma separated)

3. Choose **Booking Mode**: "Instant Booking"
4. Set pricing:
   - Price: Enter the full ticket price (e.g., 500)
   - Available Seats: Number of instant seats (e.g., 100)

5. Upload an image of your experience
6. Click "Create Experience"

#### Option B: Reservation with Hold (Token-Based)
Best for: Dining, Temples, Workshops where users reserve first then confirm

1. Go to **Your Experiences** → **Create Experience**
2. Fill in basic details (same as above)
3. Choose **Booking Mode**: "Reservation Only"
4. Set pricing:
   - Token Amount: Amount to hold the seat (e.g., 100)
   - Hold Duration: How long the reservation lasts
     - 10 minutes: Quick confirmations
     - 1 hour: Standard dining reservations
     - 6 hours: Workshops, classes
     - 24 hours: Major events

5. Set capacity: Total seats available
6. Click "Create Experience"

#### Option C: Both Modes
Best for: Flexibility - Let users choose whether to book instantly or reserve

1. Choose **Booking Mode**: "Both Instant & Reservation"
2. Set both pricing options:
   - Instant Price: Full payment price
   - Token Amount: Reservation token
   - Hold Duration: Reservation validity period
   - Instant Availability: How many seats for instant booking
3. Regular capacity will be for reservations
4. Click "Create Experience"

### Step 4: Monitor Your Bookings

#### Dashboard
- Shows real-time stats
- Recent bookings listed
- Quick overview of performance

#### Manage Experiences Tab
- View all your experiences
- See booking count
- Check approval status
- Edit or view details

#### Bookings Tab
- See all customer bookings
- Filter by status
- View customer details
- Check-in customers
- Export reports

### Step 5: Validate Bookings (Check-in)

For events where you need to verify attendance:

1. Go to **Bookings** tab
2. Find the booking record
3. Click "Check-in" button
4. Or scan QR code from booking confirmation

This confirms the customer attended and completes the booking.

## Tips & Best Practices

### 📸 Image Tips
- Use clear, attractive images
- Minimum size: 400x300 pixels
- Show the actual experience
- Update seasonally

### 💰 Pricing Tips
- Research competitor pricing
- Token amount: Usually 10-20% of full price
- Be competitive but fair
- Consider time-based pricing variations

### 📝 Description Tips
- Be clear and specific
- List actual highlights
- Mention any restrictions (age, dress code)
- Highlight unique features
- Include cancellation policy information

### 🎯 Capacity Tips
- Be realistic about capacity
- Reserve some seats for on-the-day bookings
- Update regularly as you understand demand
- Peak seasons vs off-season planning

### ⏰ Hold Duration Tips
- 10 mins: For high-turnover venues
- 1 hour: Standard for dining/activities
- 6 hours: For day-use activities
- 24 hours: For major events

## Common Questions

### Q: How long does experience approval take?
**A:** Usually 2-4 hours during business hours. Check your email for status updates.

### Q: Can I change pricing after creating?
**A:** Yes! Go to **Experiences** → **Edit** → Save changes. Changes take effect immediately.

### Q: What if someone doesn't show up?
**A:** You can cancel their booking from the Bookings page. They'll get a refund based on your cancellation policy.

### Q: How do I get paid?
**A:** Payments are settled weekly to your registered bank account. See **Settlements** tab for details.

### Q: Can I add more capacity later?
**A:** Yes! Go to **Experiences** → **Edit** → Update capacity → Save.

### Q: How do reservations work after hold expires?
**A:** If the customer doesn't confirm within the hold period:
- Reservation auto-cancels
- Customer (if they want) joins the waitlist
- Seat becomes available again
